# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is eUNC?

Enhanced UNC Benchmark is a behavior-based Roblox executor capability benchmark.

## What does eUNC test?

eUNC evaluates registered tests designed to examine executor capabilities and their behavior.

The exact coverage depends on the benchmark version.

## Does a high score mean an executor is safe?

No.

A high score does not certify executor safety, trustworthiness, or malware-free behavior.

## Does eUNC guarantee every UNC function works?

No.

Passing tests only demonstrates the behavior evaluated by those tests.

## Why does my executor fail a test even though the function exists?

Function presence does not guarantee correct behavior.

However, a failed test may also indicate an incorrect assertion or an environment-specific limitation.

## What does UNKNOWN mean?

UNKNOWN means the test could not conclusively classify the observed behavior.

It should not be treated as PASS.

## Why does eUNC identify my executor incorrectly?

Executor identification depends on the identification methods and information available in the environment.

Identification is informational and is not a cryptographic verification of executor identity.

## Why are emojis missing?

Some executor consoles may not support displaying Unicode symbols correctly.

## Can I save results?

Yes, when the required file-writing and JSON encoding capabilities are available.

The benchmark can export a JSON report when saving is enabled.

## Can I modify tests?

You can contribute improvements and corrections.

However, modifying tests to manufacture successful results defeats the purpose of the benchmark.
